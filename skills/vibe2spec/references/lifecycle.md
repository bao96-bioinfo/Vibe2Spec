# Project history, acceptance, and reopening

Resume from the explicitly selected project record and its exact referenced contents. Validate those references and inspect the actual response and review provenance. Identify the accepted revision of each finished step, the first unfinished current step, pending choices, missing reviews, and suspended dependencies. Keep conflicting or missing evidence unresolved. A worker's claim that a step is done cannot replace acceptance.

The ordered project has one current step. Later steps remain provisional until the current step passes its required checks, separate results review, and explicit user acceptance. An unaffected previously finished step can remain finished after an earlier step reopens. A project is finished only when all required steps have current valid acceptance and no open necessary amendment remains.

## Preserve reviewed content

Preserve exact bytes before presenting a decision, specification, or prepared work for approval or review. Save a snapshot with the SHA-256 fingerprint and byte length of every scoped file. A fingerprint identifies contents; it does not authenticate consent. Keep the preserved files available. Do not change a presented draft or frozen record in place. Create its next editable revision and preserve the original response or review with the original snapshot.

The project index and a mutable progress tracker may point to newer records. They are navigation aids, not approval evidence. Approved task documents keep their original checkbox contents. Progress appears in later evidence, so ticking a box cannot change the approved specification's fingerprint.

## Accept a result

Present the exact specification revision, reviewed implementation, runs, comparisons, outputs, and results review when requesting acceptance. Summarize passed checks and their tested scope. Identify the artifacts that will become protected and the next provisional step. An acceptance response is meaningful only when its scope identifies those exact results.

If a required check failed or a review has unresolved findings, retain the result as unfinished even when the user says it looks acceptable. Explain the outstanding condition and ask for its resolution through the specification process. Do not fabricate a pass or silently revise criteria. A legitimate revised criterion receives a new approval and fresh applicable verification; the original failure remains historical.

## Reopen only necessary changes

A necessary amendment identifies a blocking requirement or defect that prevents correct project progress. Explain what happens if the accepted work stays unchanged. A preference for refactoring or style alone does not establish necessity.

Prepare an amendment before editing protected work. Inventory every affected script, specification, configuration, decision, input, output, and check. For each path, state `create`, `modify`, or `remove`, the exact planned change, and its necessity. Identify affected downstream steps through direct and transitive dependencies. Name superseded decisions and unchanged decisions retained with their original provenance.

Preserve this proposal and obtain approval of the exact scope. Create a new revision of the corresponding step and make it current. Preserve previously accepted files at their original paths. New preserved records use new paths. Suspend affected downstream work and retain its old evidence as historical for the old inputs. Unaffected finished steps remain closed.

Reopening approval authorizes the disclosed revision process. It does not settle unspecified methods or accept future results. Interview the affected decisions, approve and separately review the revised specification, prepare code, separately review it, execute, check results, obtain results review, and request explicit acceptance again.

When another affected path or operation is discovered, stop that expanded work and obtain approval for the expanded inventory. After the reopened step is accepted, renew downstream checks, reviews, and acceptance where their assumptions changed. Resume only when each affected dependency has current valid evidence.
